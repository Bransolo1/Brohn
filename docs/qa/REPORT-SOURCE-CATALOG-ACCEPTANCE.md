## Reproduce and continue

Use the [six-test guide](../../tests/REPORT-SOURCE-CATALOG-REPRODUCE.md), explicit
checkout/output arguments and fresh disposable outputs. The generic runner does
not supply those arguments. These commands do not reproduce private browser
fixtures or the process supervisor.

**Restart complete local services after updating.** Do not replace source under
an already registered participant process or rewrite historical descriptors.

Large cardiac history, exact downloads/cold reopening, responsive background
reading and unattended report preparation remain open in the
[implementation contract](../architecture/REPORT-RESPONSIVENESS-AND-CONTINUATION.md).
No package/capability is completed; scientific, device and hosting gates remain.
