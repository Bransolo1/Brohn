## Preserved failures and open gates

Earlier failures remain failures, including deadline/admission/fixture failures and the original duplicate cardiac table-region labels. Historical artifacts retain their original bytes and any recorded accessibility finding; new rendering fixes do not relabel an old file as passing. Narrow caption/component qualification is not full UI acceptance.

The combined19 report job took 276.789 seconds under its existing 300-second deadline. The earlier mixed job took 289.47 seconds. Combined19 saved-history open took 80.342 seconds. These workload observations leave real performance work; they are neither broad capacity/concurrency guarantees nor a controlled before/after benchmark. A later separately checked reader optimization was not automatically joined into this source.

Still open: complete cardiac researcher-browser and fresh-process reopening, responsive history/download/editing, wider workload capacity, option-level scientific appraisal, physical-device agreement, confirmed NN-HRV support, further physiological adapters and original raw-recording bundles. No platform capability is marked complete merely by this package summary.
