## Scope and authority

The metadata helper uses current eligible heads, exact saved audio/report and
lineage references, current and historical project ownership, and explicit
authorization of the requested project. It does not decode every scientific
result in R. A row listing is not authorization to open or download its source;
the original complete reader and guarded native resources remain authoritative.

Pages keep a first-read immutable-version watermark and date/id keyset. New
insertions, including tied and backdated dates, wait for Show latest. Newly
revised current heads also wait; previous heads are never resurrected or
silently replaced. Revoked or archived entries disappear and current eligible
counts can decrease. The helper makes no catalog/schema/index writes.

## Limits

This is software-generated source evidence and source-bound saved-view
navigation. It establishes neither physical hardware synchronization nor
scientific validity of a measure. Only original container clock mapping is
shown. Current access may hide earlier rows; the UI must not imply an immutable
count of all historical revisions. General large-catalog performance remains a
separate qualification.
