gaze/questionnaire/paired study used the unchanged maximum of 100 figures.

All 38 original stored objects and 18 original jobs remained unchanged; only one
display prerequisite and two assembly attempts were added, and all 21 jobs were
terminal. The original 127-file workspace and 1,337-file candidate were unchanged.

This evidence is API/worker scoped. It does not claim an actual browser
panel-limit interaction. The two prior test attempts are retained: one omitted
methods-Python setup; another used a record-only comparison on a data.frame after
the real refusal had passed. Both were harness defects; the fresh corrected run
used a frozen script and exited successfully.

## Scientific and source invariants

- Original scores, saved exclusions, uncertainty, support and scoring recipes
  remain immutable. No scorer/importer/cohort reducer runs during display export.
- Native display preparation may replay the retained journal once under its
  pinned preparation identity. Assembly and historical opening consume the
  resulting immutable artifact without replay or scientific recomputation.
- Imported trial summaries stay declared summaries. They do not become native
  event evidence. Unselected source rows and raw lexical values remain complete.
- GNAT unavailable metrics stay null; withholding is distinct from missing data.
  Unlinked cohort person denominators stay null, not invented zero-person results.
- Preparation, renderer and scientific producer identities remain distinct.
  Exact saved references and fresh source/current-reader checks govern history.
- Fewer figures never remove complete selected numerical evidence. Unsupported
  scientific collections fail with a reason rather than disappearing.

Earlier backend/source fixture and harness failures remain in their external
receipts; passing assertions before a trailing parse failure were not accepted
as a completed run. Backend05 is the accepted terminal backend phase. The later
exact-prepared selector correction has its own 11-check copied-source evidence.

labelled keyboard-focusable horizontal scrollers and minimum readable chart
width; final Journey05 and its actual downloaded HTML were checked again. A
prior Unicode region-label separator was replaced with ASCII after a Windows

source-bound ordinal move with identical role, media type, multiplicity and
bytes; ten negative boundary checks passed. The earlier failing receipt remains;
no application or scientific source changed for that correction.
