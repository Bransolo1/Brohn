# Open decisions and evidence

The [master architecture](MASTER-ARCHITECTURE.md) and
[large-build readiness](preparation/LARGE-BUILD-READINESS.md) define current scope.
The toolkit is prepared; the 17 build packages remain planned and the application
still has the 01A–01L behavior recorded in STATUS.md.

- [Full lifecycle and storage](product/STUDY-LIFECYCLE.md) and
  [portable designs](product/DESIGN-PORTABILITY.md) are now explicit specifications.
  The app still has local draft saves and limited matching-report reopen, not the
  planned dataset/history/template catalog, general clone, portable-package import,
  participant deployment/enrollment or complete backup/restore service. UX-J19–J34
  and their package owners track those gaps. No remote collection is implied by
  the local loopback URL or by source publication to GitHub.

- No undergraduate interviews/formative sessions have been run; target persona
  and action budgets come from the user's brief and researched design plan.
- No reference eye/EEG/EDA/AAT rig access or SDK redistribution eligibility confirmed.
- The selected BIAT procedure now has an explicit fast-trial denominator and
  scoring order in methods/reuse/IMPLICIT-REUSE.md. Independent author-data replay,
  correction-inclusive runner timing and the exact physical AAT profile remain
  specific implementation/reference tasks; no operational scorer is exposed yet.
- The [50-capability register](preparation/capability-register.json) now covers
  webcam respiration/pulse, voice, body/hand motion, fNIRS, advanced neural and
  broader cognitive/choice methods. Scope and isolated prototypes do not establish
  qualified device/provider support or a validated combined emotion/attention score.
- ECG/PPG/HRV, general/facial EMG, startle and respiration now belong to BWP09 in
  the current build manifest; pupil processing belongs to BWP07. Historical tickets
  B195/B196/B233 remain archive references. Method specifications are prepared;
  wearable/VR-specific acquisition and coordinate support remain distinct work.
- Isolated method references now include 81 earlier and 101 additional assertions,
  synthetic model inference and assisted segmentation. Product import-to-report
  workers, reference-labelled gaze/beat/artifact checks, automated AOI workflows
  and inferential UI remain to implement; model smoke checks do not establish accuracy.
- SC-IAT templates require an exact source-versioned scorer binding before
  analytical activation. Do not adopt a hybrid formula from unrelated IAT defaults.
- Manual AOIs, draft prepared-interval arithmetic, fictional sample Results and
  a canonical prepared CSV CLI now exist. Raw device conversion, fixation detection,
  observed trial/exposure registries and inferential recipes are not yet integrated
  or qualified in the product. Planned registries and fixed protocol snapshots exist;
  binding sessions/events to them and participant execution remain next increments.
- Control/test designation and order previews are implemented; neutral/multiple
  controls, method-specific baseline/practice/check phases, persistent order
  assignment and observed timing remain in methods/CONTROL-DESIGN.md.
- Prepared CSV parsing is bounded by source bytes/rows, not peak memory. A local
  single-user import/save/reopen flow has lower limits; job isolation, historical
  artifact browsing, durable queues and role-based access remain future work.

Source pointers for the latest comparison: iMotions module catalogue and Fall2025
update; Noldus FaceReader technical specifications; RealEye behavioural-research
overview; Tobii multimodal research page. Existing citations remain in the archive.

Established-method reuse is now the default build route; see methods/reuse/README.md
for exact tested versions and API/default issues. FaceReader/Affectiva need exact
entitled SDK/API inputs; Hume's current facial endpoint availability was not
confirmed. MediaPipe face/pose/hand negative-frame checks and synthetic segmentation
now run locally; Py-Feat and entitled facial-expression providers are not integrated.
The newer MediaPipe Windows interactive segmenter fails at a DLL symbol; an
isolated 0.10.21 worker with its matching model passes the recorded smoke checks.
NeuroKit's experimental video_ppg is not an adopted webcam pulse implementation.
The tested local environments are not yet durable Brohn job workers.

The new brand preview passes automated contrast and desktop/narrow axe checks;
brand integration, full workflow/manual accessibility and participant-facing
appearance isolation still need implementation and evaluation. The 18 UX journeys
are planned acceptance scenarios, not completed undergraduate studies. Introduction
and launch materials remain a plan; no outreach, publication or usage metrics exist.
