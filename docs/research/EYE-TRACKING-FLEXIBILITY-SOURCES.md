# Eye-tracking flexibility — sources, depth and independence

Accessed 1 October 2026. These are screened primary publications or official technical sources, not a systematic review. No paper was downloaded to the workspace. Selected text was read through the web tool; “abstract” is not a full methods/results appraisal. Source summaries across this review are deliberately short. No listed source is represented as validating the exact Brohn implementation.

## What remains under-evidenced

The family map is broader than completed option-level appraisal. A1/A4 are selected supporting-text reviews; A2/A5/A6/A7/A9 remain abstract-level, A3/A8 have limited indexed text, and V1–V3 are technical documentation. Menu reading, target-absent search/censoring, dynamic tracking, longitudinal exposures and product-use acquisition require further primary-method and independent assessment before strong guidance. Source counts are not qualification. Search leads with inaccessible/unverified text were not promoted into support.
