Depth summary: six publisher selected-text reviews (S4/S5/S6/S8/S9/S10), two indexed primary supporting-text reviews with access limits (S3/S11), and three abstract/metadata-only sources (S1/S2/S7). Multiple publications in a family do not remove its unresolved exact-implementation and device-transfer gaps.

## Contradictions and unresolved support

- Detector rankings differ across implementations, settings, event/sample criteria and datasets (S2–S4). This appraisal does not pool them or resolve a universal winner; S3's ranking is not treated as a controlled replication of S4.
- Noise robustness from larger regions (S6) and overlapping spatial-distribution costs (S7) are conditional findings, not a single universal AOI-size rule. S7's limited review depth constrains precise recommendations.
- No reviewed source validates the exact Brohn both-endpoint assignment, centroid-owned complete duration, six-state latency policy or blocked-adjacency transition rule. Their definitions are code findings; any scientific advantages remain prospective.
- No reviewed source supplies a universal pupil baseline duration/coverage or complete blink-detector validation. Gaze reporting guidance cannot substitute for those sources.
- This is not a systematic search or full risk-of-bias appraisal. No independent target consumer/device experiment, dynamic AOI qualification, actual webcam tracking validation or inference from looking to liking is claimed.
