These authored appraisals connect reviewed literature to concrete Brohn calculations and consequential options. They supplement the earlier inventory. They preserve each review's actual source version, reading depth, unresolved questions and independent-review limits. They are not a systematic review, an activated comprehensive registry or a declaration that a method/device has been scientifically qualified.

Follow the cited original sources within each appraisal. Abstract-only leads, selected-text reading and full-method reproduction are different evidence levels. Shared datasets/authors and contradictory findings are retained. A guideline plus another publication does not by itself validate Brohn's implementation or transfer to a new sensor, population or consumer task.

The next application work is to bind these findings to individual controls, concise explanations and saved evidence. New scientific calculations require a versioned recipe and explicit rerun. Historical records retain their original settings, results and reference identities. HRV/PRV, skin temperature, implicit tasks, questionnaires, camera/face and other implemented/planned modalities remain part of the broader coverage requirement.

[ORIGINS.json](ORIGINS.json) records exact authored-file hashes and original local audit manifests. Source-code locations and local packet names inside retained documents identify the inspected historical implementation; they are provenance descriptions, not instructions to fetch or execute files. No downloaded papers, datasets, model weights or participant records are bundled here.
