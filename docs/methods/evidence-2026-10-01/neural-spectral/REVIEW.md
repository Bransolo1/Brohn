## Concrete findings and next-revision work

1. **Queue the registry ratio wording correction.** In `registry/method-evidence-0.2.json`, entry `eeg-relative-denominator`, `claim.consumer_question` asks “What fraction of this specified spectrum lies in the chosen band?” The worker admits numerator bands and the reference denominator independently. The general wording should be: **“How does power in the chosen band compare with power in the declared reference band?”** Explain that the saved estimate is numerator-band power divided by reference-band power; it can exceed one for non-nested custom bands. Default bands are nested in the default 1–45 Hz reference, so this is not evidence of a faulty default formula. Preserve registry02 and all saved values; the change belongs in a distinct registry revision. The saved `proportion` unit is another presentation caveat for non-nested bands, not permission to rewrite historical scientific schemas.
2. **Retain the subtraction-floor caveat prominently.** `neural.py:373–383` calculates `eligible = base > minimum_power_uv2`, then applies the mask after every enabled transform, including subtraction. This is an implemented support rule, not a mathematical denominator requirement for subtraction. The literature reviewed does not establish the chosen floor. A future UI explanation must not describe this solely as protection against division by zero.
3. **Keep power and phase scopes separate.** `neural.py:358–367` accumulates phase vectors from each original trial before optionally computing power on the trial minus its condition mean. ITC therefore remains original-total-signal ITC under induced power. The two-contributor threshold is computational admission, not reliability or sample-size qualification; the code performs no count-bias correction. Labels must not imply that the ITC is induced or that subtracting evoked power removes the phase caveats.
4. **Make the direction of contrary guidance explicit when activating evidence.** Keil §3.1.2 (band power) does more than request careful relative-power interpretation: its guidance discourages relative-power estimation and requests the full spectrum when it is used. The frozen appraisal's cautious summary is directionally compatible, but future guidance should not turn that source into a default endorsement. This is not a mandate to remove preserved results or silently replace the estimator. [Keil et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/psyp.14052)
5. **Retain registered-report deviations in any deeper Rawls appraisal.** Its §4.5 reports adding evoked-potential subtraction on reviewer request and narrowing the gambling-condition analysis after preliminary value-dimension results. Calling the article a registered report is accurate; calling all analyzed choices preregistered would not be. §2.1 supports final samples of 64 and 44 and says three flanker datasets were added; §4.5 says two were added. Attribute the final sizes to §2.1 without silently reconciling the prose inconsistency or inferring a data defect. No numerical result from that paper is an acceptance threshold for Brohn. [Rawls and Sponheim](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/psyp.70073)

No static blocker was found in keeping this appraisal inactive. The wording corrections above are queued, not activated.

## Source verification and independence

The following verification concerns only the locators and claims stated here. It is not a full-paper systematic review, replication, preregistration audit or data-level independence audit. Publisher/author-institution sources were used. Intermittent publisher retrieval errors are recorded in SOURCES.json; indexed primary text was used where a direct fetch failed.

- **Keil2022:** Publisher metadata and author list match. Selected §§3.1.1–3.1.2 and reporting-checklist text support normalization, full-spectrum and smoothing/baseline reporting cautions. The relative-power recommendation is in §3.1.2, not the preceding normalization subsection. Cohen, Fabiani and Gratton occur in its author list; Cohen2019 and Gyurkovics2021 are consequently not independent author-group corroboration of that consensus. Some later full-page fetches timed out, so this review does not independently reread every original Table2–4 locator. [Publisher](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/psyp.14052)
- **Grandchamp2011:** Publisher PDF methods distinguish normalization within each full trial before the final conventional baseline from Brohn's average-power-first path. Its methods/simulation and visual-task evidence supports documenting normalization order and outlier sensitivity, not a universal preference or the subtraction floor. Authors are distinct from the other reviewed teams. [Publisher PDF](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00236/pdf?isPublishedV2=false)
- **Gyurkovics2021:** The author-hosted paper matches metadata and describes a resting-data investigation plus simulations under an additive model. Its discussion supports model-dependent distortions from divisive/dB correction and checking spectral background differences. It does not prove every dataset is additive. Fabiani and Gratton overlap Keil. [University-hosted paper](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/96237/1/Gyurkovics_etal_2021_NeuroImage.pdf)
- **van Diepen2018:** Publisher PDF equation3, ERP and power/SNR simulations, and discussion support the phase-interpretation caution. It demonstrates possible ITPC changes without the intended ongoing-phase change; it does not test all task designs or this exact MNE kernel. The authors are distinct from the other appraised groups. [Publisher PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-20423-z.pdf)
- **Rawls2025:** Publisher §§2.1,2.4–2.5,4.4–4.5 support the 64/44 deployed-veteran samples, STFT and parameterization differences, and limitations/deviations. Distinct authors and empirical data add relevant context; no direct consumer or Brohn transfer is established. [Publisher](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/psyp.70073)
- **Donoghue2020:** Publisher metadata/abstract confirm the periodic/aperiodic separation aim and confounding concern. Exact fitting settings and performance were not independently reviewed here. Keep abstract-only status in the original packet. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-020-00744-x)
- **Cohen2019:** PubMed abstract and publisher abstract/section snippets confirm the FWHM reporting rationale. This follow-up encountered additional snippets; it does not retroactively upgrade the original packet's abstract-only depth or establish exact kernel equivalence. Cohen also authored Keil. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31145982/), [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811919304409)
- **Welch1967:** Indexed IBM author-institution metadata/abstract confirm modified-periodogram averaging and method origin. The direct fetch failed; supporting derivations and exact EEG parameter adequacy remain unreviewed. [IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/publications/the-use-of-fast-fourier-transform-for-the-estimation-of-power-spectra-a-method-based-on-time-averaging-over-short-modified-periodograms)

Multiple relevant sources improve coverage but do not establish source independence at every level. Method developers, overlapping authors, shared theoretical assumptions and unrelated datasets must not be counted as independent exact-option replications. None of these sources establishes a consumer engagement, emotion or preference score for the current recipes.
