# Academic evidence audit — 30 September 2026

This is a bounded code and literature audit for Brohn, **not completed scientific
qualification**. It maps actual analysis settings to research questions and
evidence gaps, with no changes to calculations, saved results or research claims.
Read the [governing evidence contract](../ACADEMIC-EVIDENCE-CONTRACT.md).

- [Actual methods and options](OPTION-INVENTORY.md): 30 inventory rows, each with
  several consequential settings still needing individual review.
- [Measurement evidence](EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md): 24 families and 57 screened scholarly
  starting links. The machine-readable companion preserves screening scope.
- [Required improvements](ACTIONABLE-DEPARTURES.md): ten concrete topics spanning
  algorithms, context transfers, terminology and study starters.
- [Study design](study-design/EVIDENCE.md): 12 topics, with a
  [32-source bibliography](study-design/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) and
  [five proposed design profiles](study-design/TEMPLATE-PREFLIGHT.md).
- [Registry and researcher guidance](REGISTRY-RECOMMENDATION.md): the proposed
  exact option/context/claim/evidence bindings and historical preservation rules.

The two bibliographies overlap; do not add their counts and call that the number
of independent sources. Most sources were screened through abstracts or selected
sections. Full-text option appraisal, conflicting evidence, independence and
applicability review remain necessary. Two design references retain metadata-only
status. No source count automatically approves a procedure.

certificate. Implementation, reference calculations, physical acquisition,
psychometrics and consumer validity need their own scoped evidence.
