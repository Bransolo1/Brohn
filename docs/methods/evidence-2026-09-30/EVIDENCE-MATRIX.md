# Measurement evidence — screened starting matrix

Accessed 30 September 2026. These are candidate evidence links for the actual versions/options in [OPTION-INVENTORY.md](OPTION-INVENTORY.md). **No row closes the option-level review.** Multiple papers about a family do not validate every option, implementation, population, device or consumer interpretation.

Roles: **M** method/procedure or reporting guidance; **V** empirical validation or measurement limitations; **C** consumer applicability. “V” always refers to the cited study's tested conditions, never automatic Brohn validation. Consensus, methods tutorials and reviews are identified separately from primary experiments. Author overlap and shared datasets must be assessed before claiming independent corroboration. Most screening was bibliographic/abstract or selected sections; full-text appraisal remains necessary for parameter decisions.

## Contemporary consumer applicability: a separate layer

[Bigne et al. 2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mar.70002) is a contemporary methods framework connecting marketing aims, stimuli, measures and consumer processes. Use it to organize research questions and distinguish direct measurement, cross-measure association and predictive/explanatory aims. It is not a universal mapping from physiology to psychological labels.

[Venkatraman et al. 2015](https://scholarship.miami.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Predicting-Advertising-success-beyond-Traditional-Measures/991032069208202976) is primary consumer research comparing traditional, implicit, eye-tracking, biometric, EEG and fMRI measures for 30-second television ads. It provides a concrete example of testing incremental consumer relevance, not a guarantee that all measures improve every prediction.

[Hartnett et al., How to accurately measure attention to video advertising](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/02650487.2024.2435164) (online 2024; issue 2025) is a current primary-study lead for comparing attention measures. Bibliographic and partial publisher-text screening only; study design, result details and limits still need full extraction before any option is marked supported. No numerical or superiority claim is taken from it here.

Consumer evidence remains especially incomplete for Brohn's exact skin-temperature thresholds, belt/volume respiration transfer, generic EMG, movement features, browser keyboard AAT, model-specific face/attention outputs, fNIRS preprocessing and spontaneous audio. The matrix retains these families rather than substituting citations from another modality or claiming them validated because they run.

## Explicitly unclosed branches

The capability register includes EOG, rPPG, advanced hemodynamics/neural methods, naturalistic/dynamic gaze, VAAST/manikin, stop-signal and other agile/relational tasks. They require their own current implementation discovery and method/validation/consumer sources. This pass does not satisfy multiple-source coverage for each such branch. The [option inventory](OPTION-INVENTORY.md) keeps them as scope items, and the separate `study-design/` audit handles template/design-level literature. A later review must also inspect newer contrary findings, publication corrections, instrument translations, relevant population diversity and exact default/parameter choices.
