Product name: **Brohn**. Use this name throughout the larger build.

Build a guided workspace where a first-time psychology student can prepare a
controlled study, collect linked measures, recover from interruptions and explain
an automatically produced result. Retain commercial reuse, multimodal breadth,
rich questionnaires and automation as the product destination.

## Review outcome

The architecture remains R domain/science, Shiny researcher UI, separate browser
participant execution, acquisition sidecars and isolated workers. No frontend
rewrite is needed. Independent reviews found five sequencing corrections:

1. Bind observed sessions/events to frozen protocols before playback.
2. Give repeated exposures, questionnaires and sensors shared identity/phase
   semantics; the prepared CSV cannot become a multimodal interchange format.
3. Implement durable assignment, event delivery, collection finalization and jobs
   with execution before promising automatic completion.
4. Bring questionnaire execution/linkage into the first eye-study journey, then
   grow its type catalogue on tested semantics.
5. Include novice testing/accessibility/effort measurement in each wave and static
   AOI proposals in the first complete eye-study workflow.
